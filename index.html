      In the Details view, drag over text with the Highlight or Mark private tool. Private marks are saved in the PDF as annotations that pseudonymizing tools can find, and the Privacy list button saves them as JSON. With the mouse: drag pages to reorder them. Ctrl-click or Shift-click selects several. On a touch screen, tap to select and press and hold to drag.
